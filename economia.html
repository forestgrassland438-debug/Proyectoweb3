Economy & treasuries

Two treasuries. The circular-economy treasury pays the coins monsters drop; the game treasury keeps the game's income. Shop and P2P fees are 5%: 1.5% circular and 3.5% game. Exchange fees go to the circular treasury; map travel, VIP and revive potions go to the game treasury.

Supply, burns and the price index

Active money = players + circular treasury. Total supply = every coin, treasuries included. Net inflation subtracts the value of destroyed items (trash, expired drops): they can no longer be sold to the shop for new coins. Shop, death, smelting and crafting prices follow the price index.
